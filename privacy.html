Privacy Policy for Salatuk First

Effective Date: October 2025

Thank you for using Salatuk First (the “App”). Your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information, permissions, and data access within the App.

1. Information We Collect

Salatuk First does not collect, store, or share personal information such as names, emails, or phone numbers. The app functions entirely on your device and does not send any data to external servers.

2. Permissions We Use

  • Location Permission: Used to determine your city or region in order to calculate accurate prayer times. Location data is processed locally and never transmitted or stored externally.
  • Notification Permission: Used to send reminders and countdowns for prayer times. No personal or device data is shared through notifications.
  • Usage Data Access Permission: Used only to detect whether the screen is active or the device is in use, helping the app schedule notifications appropriately. We do not store or transmit this information.
  • Appear on Top (Overlay) Permission: Allows the app to show prayer reminders or overlays while you are using other apps. This permission is used solely for display purposes and does not collect any information about the apps running beneath the overlay.

3. Data Security

Since the app does not store any personal data, the risk of unauthorized access is minimal. All calculations and permissions operate locally on your device.

4. Children’s Privacy

Salatuk First is safe for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 years old.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will always be available on this page with a new effective date.
